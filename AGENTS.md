- Maintain one semantic component tree with `quiet` and `pastel` visual variants. Do not fork full pages or long-lived design branches.

- Public invitation copy and approved photo metadata are runtime-managed through `/admin/content`: D1 stores immutable revisions and publication pointers, while private R2 stores immutable original/480/960 image keys. Never publish every keystroke; preserve explicit draft, preview, and publish states, keep the bundled content as a public read fallback, and fail closed for production admin writes when D1, R2, or a verified Cloudflare Access JWT is unavailable.
